# Factor scoring rubric

Score: +2 strong positive · +1 positive · 0 neutral / not found · −1 negative · −2 strong negative

## Long-term lens

### 1. Company financials
- +2: Revenue and PAT growing >20% CAGR over 3 yrs, positive operating cash flow, debt/equity < 0.5, stable or rising margins
- +1: Growth 10–20%, cash flow mostly positive, manageable debt
- 0: Flat or mixed
- −1: Profit falling, or cash flow negative while profit is positive (quality concern)
- −2: Losses with no clear path, or debt/equity > 2, or sudden profit jump only in the IPO year (window dressing)

### 2. IPO valuation
Compare issue P/E (on post-issue diluted EPS) with 2–4 listed peers.
- +2: >20% cheaper than peer median with similar or better growth
- +1: Roughly in line with peers
- 0: Can't find peers / loss-making (use P/S or EV/EBITDA instead and note it)
- −1: 20–50% premium to peers
- −2: >50% premium without clearly superior growth

### 6. Promoters, OFS and use of funds
- +2: Mostly fresh issue for capex / debt repayment, promoter holding stays >50%, clean record
- +1: Mixed fresh + OFS, reasonable use of funds
- 0: Unclear
- −1: Mostly OFS (>70%), or big "general corporate purposes" bucket
- −2: 100% OFS by PE funds exiting at high valuation, or pending SEBI/legal cases, related-party concerns

### 8. Sector trend (extra)
- +2: Sector index outperforming Nifty over 3–6 months, recent sector IPOs listed well
- +1: Sector steady
- 0: Neutral
- −1: Sector underperforming
- −2: Sector in sharp downtrend or facing regulatory hit

### 9. Anchor investors & lock-ins (extra)
- +2: Reputed long-only funds (large MFs, insurers, sovereign funds) as anchors
- +1: Decent anchor book
- 0: No anchors (normal for SME) / not found
- −1: Anchors mostly unknown names
- −2: Large pre-IPO allotments at much lower prices with short lock-ins

## Listing-gain lens

### 3. Subscription demand
Use final (or latest) times-subscribed by category. QIB matters most.
- +2: QIB > 50x and overall > 50x
- +1: QIB 10–50x
- 0: QIB 2–10x
- −1: QIB 1–2x or just scraped through
- −2: Under-subscribed or QIB < 1x
SME: no QIB in many cases — use NII and overall, and cap at +1 (SME demand is easier to inflate).

### 4. Overall market sentiment
Nifty / Sensex and sector index over last 5–10 sessions, India VIX, FII flows.
- +2: Indices near highs, VIX low, recent IPOs listing strongly
- +1: Stable
- 0: Sideways
- −1: Recent correction, rising VIX
- −2: Sharp sell-off during IPO week

### 5. Grey Market Premium (GMP)
Use GMP % = GMP / upper price band. Look at the trend, not one number.
- +2: GMP > 40% and stable or rising
- +1: 15–40%
- 0: 0–15% or not available
- −1: Falling sharply, or near zero
- −2: Negative
Always label GMP "unofficial". For SME IPOs, never score above +1.

### 7. Listing-day supply and demand
Before listing: estimate from retail oversubscription (high retail → many allottees may sell on listing) and short-term investor share.
After listing: use actual volume and open vs close.
- +2: Strong institutional demand, low free float on listing day
- +1: Balanced
- 0: Unknown
- −1: Very high retail/NII with weak QIB (flipping risk)
- −2: Heavy selling seen / large shares unlocking soon

### 10. Issue size (extra)
- +1: Mid-size mainboard (₹500–5,000 cr) — liquid but not oversupplied
- 0: Very large (>₹10,000 cr) — needs huge demand; or normal
- −1: Tiny SME (<₹25 cr) — high volatility, possible operator activity
(Range here is −1 to +1; that's fine.)

## Hard red flags (cap that lens at "Mixed")
- Auditor resignation, qualified audit opinion, or restated financials
- Pending SEBI order / major litigation against promoters
- Revenue heavily from related parties
- SME IPO with tiny float, huge subscription and very high GMP (classic pump risk)
- Negative operating cash flow for 3 years while reporting profits
